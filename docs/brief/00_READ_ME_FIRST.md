## Package for the unsuspecting Sage

**Working project:** Independent procedural spaceship exterior generator (future potential NoorMD bench: **NoorShip**).

**Scope:** Generate plausible, diverse, chunky, industrial hard-surface spaceship *exteriors*. Not a spaceship flight game, not a level generator, and not a NoorMD integration yet.

**Guiding sentence:** *Less elegant star yacht, more hardworking flying industrial facility.*

## How to read
1. `01_VISUAL_STYLE_BRIEF.md` — aesthetic direction, approved and rejected design language, image guidance.
2. `02_PRODUCT_DESIGN_CONCEPT.md` — what the independent tool should do, workflows, controls, deliverables, acceptance criteria.
3. `03_MODULAR_GENERATION_SYSTEM.md` — technical architecture, algorithms, module library, interfaces, validation, exporters and tests.
4. `04_SAGE_HANDOFF.md` — concise marching orders and deliberately bounded initial implementation.
5. `references/` — original Thalassa submarine concept and three new **mood/style** spaceship concepts. These are inspiration, **not** dimensioned specifications or meshes.

## Important project boundaries
- Remain independent, engine agnostic, deterministic and usable without NoorMD.
- Support GLB first; keep engine-specific export adapters optional.
- Future integration into NoorMD/NoorShip is *a possibility*, not a reason to entangle the proof of concept with its codebase.
- Don't assume Anubis or BadLevel is part of this implementation. Ship-interior integration can be an optional later extension through structured metadata.
- Author with functional constraints: readable silhouette, access/docking points, engine clearance, modular geometry, performance and artist editability.
- Procedural does not mean random greebles: large forms first, details last.
